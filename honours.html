Notable records

  • Youngest player to score in a World Cup final since Pelé (1958) — achieved at the 2018 final.
  • One of only two players in history to score a hat-trick in a World Cup final, alongside Geoff Hurst (1966).
  • Among the most expensive teenage transfers in football history following his move to PSG.
  • Became PSG’s all-time leading goalscorer, surpassing Edinson Cavani.
  • Rose to become one of France’s all-time top international goalscorers.
